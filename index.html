FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need special hardware to start?
No. QR check-in works with any phone or tablet from day one. NFC cards, turnstiles, and door controllers are optional add-ons when you're ready to gate entry automatically.
How do members pay me?
Online payments run through your own Billplz account, so FPX and DuitNow transfers land directly in your bank. Cash and terminal payments can be recorded at the counter too.
How does the WhatsApp automation work?
You scan one QR code to connect your business WhatsApp number. From then on, booking confirmations, class reminders, and renewal nudges send automatically from your own number.
Can I move my existing members over?
Yes. Bring your member list in a spreadsheet and we'll help you import members, remaining sessions, and expiry dates so nobody loses what they've paid for.
Is there a contract or lock-in?
No. RM99/mo, cancel anytime, and your data is always yours to export.
