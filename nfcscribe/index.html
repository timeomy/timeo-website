NFCScribe

A focused, no-nonsense utility for working with NFC tags on iPhone.

What it does

  • Reads NDEF tags (NTAG, MIFARE Ultralight, ISO 15693) and shows text, URLs, raw bytes, and the tag UID
  • Writes text or URL records to writable NDEF tags
  • Keeps a private, on-device history of every read and write
  • Zero network requests — your tag data never leaves your iPhone

Requirements

iPhone 7 or later, iOS 17 or newer. Not available on iPad, Mac, or Vision Pro (no NFC hardware).
