Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-08

NFCScribe is published by Oxloz LLC ("we", "us"). This policy describes what NFCScribe does and does not do with your information. The short version: we never see your data because the app does not communicate with any server.

What NFCScribe collects

Nothing leaves your device. NFCScribe does not have a backend. It does not contact any server we operate, any third-party analytics service, any advertising network, or any cloud sync provider. There is no sign-up, no account, and no telemetry.

The app does store the following only on your iPhone, in the app's private Documents directory, accessible only to NFCScribe:

  • Tag identifiers (UIDs) of NFC tags you scan or write
  • The NDEF records (text, URL, raw bytes) read from or written to those tags
  • Timestamps of each scan or write

This data lives in a single JSON file (Documents/history.json) inside the app's sandbox. Uninstalling NFCScribe removes this file along with the app.

What NFCScribe does NOT collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or contact information
  • Your location (the app does not request location permission)
  • Contacts, photos, calendars, microphone, camera, or any other system data
  • Any device identifier beyond what iOS exposes to a normal app
  • Crash reports, usage analytics, or behavioral data
  • Advertising identifiers (we do not run ads)

Permissions used

NFC Tag Reading — required to read and write NFC tags. iOS prompts for this when you initiate a scan; you can revoke it in Settings → NFCScribe.

That is the only permission NFCScribe asks for.

Children

NFCScribe is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from any user, including children.

Third-party services

NFCScribe does not embed any third-party SDK, analytics tool, advertising library, or crash reporter.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is no data to access, export, correct, or delete on our side. To remove all NFCScribe data from your device, uninstall the app or use the "Clear all" button in the History tab.

Changes

If we ever update this policy, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
